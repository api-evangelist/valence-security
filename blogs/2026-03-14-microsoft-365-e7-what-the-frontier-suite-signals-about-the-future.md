Microsoft's new Microsoft 365 E7 licensing bundle signals a shift toward treating AI agents as governable enterprise entities requiring identity management and security controls. As AI becomes embedded in SaaS applications and capable of autonomous action, security teams must focus on managing agent access, entitlements, and auditability rather than treating AI primarily as a productivity feature.

The Stryker cyberattack demonstrates how compromised SaaS management systems like Microsoft Entra ID and Intune can cause widespread operational disruption without traditional ransomware or malware. Security teams must recognize that trusted SaaS administration breakdowns represent critical infrastructure risks requiring rigorous access controls and monitoring across identity and management platforms.
